# The acceptance evidence, the flythrough repairs, and the numbers that moved

Session of 2026-09-17, second of the day. `main` went `6780225` → `54f2726` through `f06aaf8`, `39ca459`, `144766b`, `f160fe9`, `79ba3a3`. This entry records what was believed and proved false, what the reviews caught, and which numbers moved, so a later session does not re-derive them.

## Numbers that moved, and why the comparison is only partly like-for-like

The acceptance state's frame interval went from **p50 83.3 / p95 183.4 ms** (2026-09-16) to **p50 16.7 / p95 33.4 ms** on the same population (3,000 pedestrians, 59 vehicles drawn), with the fixed step at p50 11.8 ms against 75.7. Three things changed between the two measurements and only two are code:

- the tick lever (`42f5639`) landed between them, which is the code change;
- the old numbers were taken while a sibling checkout held the machine at 66–77% CPU, and the new ones under 12–42%, so part of the move is load rather than work;
- the new numbers were taken with the observation-only probe seam present in a worktree build (`11c5d868…`), because the seam that gives the lane its frame boundary has never existed on `main` — the committed lane cannot measure the committed build. The old numbers were taken the same way, in a worktree that has since been destroyed, so they are quoted as reported rather than re-derivable.

A later session quoting "the app holds 60 fps at 3,000 pedestrians" must carry the load condition and the seam: the median holds, the p95 slips one full vsync, and there is no margin left for a slower machine.

## Claims proved false, and where the correction lives

- **The flythrough's crowd leg does not put a crowd on screen.** The lane's purpose is to judge figure shape, interpenetration, feet and crowd-reading at 3 m. In the passing run the subject never resolves: two near-black clumps of 36×69 and 40×48 px in the top 80 rows, no limb legible at 1:1 or 6×, and a rigid-shift search over ±14 px between `crowd-008` and `crowd-011` returns dx = dy = 0 while 12.1% of the left knot's pixels change in place. The cause is arithmetic: the orbit target's height is pinned at the app's origin datum (15.200 m) while the local ground at that knot is 26.36 m, so the view axis is 34.7° down and the frame is pavement 1.9–28.8 m ahead. No input can flatten it, because panning is horizontal by construction. This is a rig/route property, not a render fault, and the prescription is a re-shoot, not an edit. (`artifacts/inspection-flythrough/REPORT-crowd.md`.)
- **The flight is not a closed loop**, though `plan.ts` said the camera ends where it opened and the last frame is comparable with the first. `ascent-010` measures 857.4 m out against the leg's own 620 m aim — 38% over — and `overview-000` is 159 m short of its 543 m aim, because the driver's per-step corrections are capped (a 0.25 rad polar step delivered 0.1322 with `settled: false`). Pixels unaffected; the claim was. (`artifacts/inspection-flythrough/REPORT-overview-ascent.md`.)
- **The ascent produces no exposure change**, though its leg description says the bloom is judged as the exposure change: exposure is constant at 1.4664071074506513 in all 22 frames. Same report.
- **The "0 in the hero frames" TAA reading was the superseded 2026-09-15 capture.** The certificate's own manifest records the opposite: all eight agent-free hero frames in `artifacts/visual/hero/hero.json` have `taaAccumulating: true`, seven at 32 samples and one at 25. The stillness fix did what it claimed; the stale reading came from the capture before it. The plan's acceptance-status paragraph was corrected, and the corrected reading matters because it is the difference between "the criterion's mechanism is present in code and absent from pixels" and "it is present in the pixels, and only the populated lanes cannot accumulate by construction".
- **The "signals not wired" reading at the populated crossing pose was a misread of a vehicle head.** The scramble maps no pedestrian signal heads at all (0 of 78 `traffic_signals`), so the old defect's shape — a pedestrian head red in its own green — cannot exist there. (`artifacts/inspection-populated/REPORT-crossing.md` §8, which marks the two stale readings superseded rather than deleting them.)

## What the reviews caught that the authors did not

- **A guard the code relied on and the tests did not.** `structuredFraction`'s relative leg was guarded only by `mean > 2`; a flat surface at mean 3 with ±1 of two-pixel-block dither scored 1.0, and deleting the guard left all 15 cases green. The guard is now `mean > 8 && deviation > 2`, the flat dark frame is a watched-red case, and `approach-005` is unchanged at 17.4%.
- **An exemption bounded by the record it exempts.** Six fixture frames marked held with zero travel returned zero failures, so a completely static camera passed as a flythrough. `judgeSequence` now takes the plan's own held-pair count per leg and fails by name on the wrong count, split runs, or a hold that is not the leg's tail.
- **A calibration figure that could not be reproduced.** The gate-proof recorded 15.3% (22 cells) for `approach-005`; the surviving bytes give 17.4% (25 cells: 6 absolute + 19 relative), which is also what that run's manifest recorded. The scratch file it rested on was deleted with its worktree. The record now cites the frame digest and the manifest agreement.
- **An over-claim about the pose ledger.** The comment said the manifest is written before the assertions; it is written before the *sequence* assertions only, and an in-loop clearance, renderer or viewport failure still aborts before it — which is exactly the failure shape that destroyed the first run's ledger and made the adjudication harder.

The methodology point worth keeping: the review's own reproduction of the structure-metric flaw was wrong on the first pass — a one-pixel ±1 checkerboard scores 0 under both rules, because the metric samples every second pixel. The flaw needed a two-pixel-block dither. A reviewer's repro is a claim to verify in both directions, the same as an author's.

## Deliberate choices a later session should not undo by accident

- **The 44-frame certificate was not re-captured on the final revision.** A re-capture replaces every frame and strands the digest-bound inspections that are the deliverable's native-resolution evidence. The certificate's build and harness bindings re-derive exactly on the final revision; its scene digest differs by the 33 restored human-agent files, which the certified frames cannot depend on because the verdict lane runs without `?agents=`. `node tools/visual/verify-output.ts --end` refuses on that difference by design, so the gate's own refusal is not evidence of a defect here.
- **F1 is not fixed.** Landing the terrain-hole fix moves the scene digest and strands the certified set; the census, the minimal fix and the gate that should own it are recorded, and the disposition belongs to the owner.
- **The probe seam is not landed on `main`.** A query-gated seam would let the frame-budget lane run against the delivered build, and it moves `dist` bytes, so it belongs with a re-certification.
