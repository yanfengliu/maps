- 2026-09-16: **The frame is not the problem, the simulation is.** At 1920x1080 with 3,000 pedestrians the GPU frame costs 0.900 ms p50 and 3.600 ms p95 against a 16.667 ms interval, and the population curve is flat from 1,000 to 4,000 walkers, so the renderer holds the target with about 13 ms of slack and the population is not the term.
