# Devlog Summary

- 2026-09-06: **Phases 2 and 3 — the placeholder is gone and real Shibuya renders, after three defects that each looked like a city** — `npm run data:scene` places MLIT's 67 3D Tiles into the world frame offline and builds PLATEAU's 2.5 m terrain TIN (183,188 triangles, 81,052 inside the box, relief 8.71 to 36.35 m, ground at the crossing 15.20 m) and its `tran` road surfaces (3,248 roads, 1,173 at LOD3, the rest draped, 79,244 triangles). All **1,740** buildings in the box are in the scene — 1,676 LOD2 and 64 LOD1, 532,315 triangles — and every one matched PLATEAU's own CityGML by `gml_id` with **zero** disagreements about height, level of detail or whether a height exists, and a **median 0.93 m** between where the pipeline placed it and where the CityGML's own roof-edge centroid puts it. **Loader: `3d-tiles-renderer`**, because the tileset already carries the LOD hierarchy and because 551.9 Mpx of texture (2,943 MB decoded) makes an eviction policy mandatory rather than optional; the ellipsoid it assumes is paid off offline, so no coordinate over about 2 km reaches the scene. **Three defects, none of which produced a blank frame or a red test.** glTF is Y-up where `CESIUM_RTC` is Z-up and nothing in the file says so — without the turn every building sat a median of **68 m** from its own batch table and the city rendered lying on its side; with the turn folded in and the tileset not declaring `gltfUpAxis: "z"` the library applied its own on top and it tilted again. A tile cache sized from the 137 MB on disk rather than the decoded bytes could not hold one 4096×4096 leaf, so the traversal deadlocked at the root and the app drew seventeen decimated buildings **while reporting itself loaded, idle and error-free** — and every pixel floor in the visual gate passed on it. And a road ring reaching past the terrain has nothing to drape onto, so PLATEAU's flat-at-zero LOD1 outlines dropped a sheet of asphalt to sea level past the edge of the world; 461 polygons now dropped. **Six of design.md's claims were wrong and are corrected in place**: the LOD1 buildings are in the same tileset, the sentinels read `null` not −9999, atlases go to 4096×4096 not 2048×2048, the count is 1,740 not 1,741 and its last digit is a property of the centroid rule, the Draco decode works, and the up-axis fact was missing entirely. One held exactly — `_zmin`/`_zmax` are orthometric, and the difference from the ellipsoidal geometry recovers the geoid undulation from the data alone at **36.786 m** against a published 36.877 m. **Two new gates, both made to go red** (`test/sentinel.test.ts` over real PLATEAU batch-table bytes, `test/placement.test.ts` over the ECEF and up-axis transforms), plus the visual gate's first check on the scene rather than the framebuffer, which caught two of the three defects above. Building textures are capped at 1024 px at load, which is what lets the whole box sit at leaf detail in 335 MB. All five gates green; all **eighteen** frames opened at 1280x720.
- 2026-09-06: **Phase 1 — the data is on disk, verified and attributed, and four plan items were wrong** — `npm run data:fetch` pulls the PLATEAU Shibuya-ku FY2025 CityGML (649,322,807 bytes, SHA-256 `f7437469…31d`, verified by length before hash), extracts the AOI members, runs Overpass (1,211,346 bytes, 7,915 records, `osm_base 2026-09-07T03:23:56Z`) and fetches the GSI cross-check tiles, all into a gitignored `data/`. Truncating the archive by 4,096 bytes made it say so and re-download. Four plan items were corrected before any data was touched: item 5 named the FY2020 Tokyo 23-ku bundle instead of Shibuya-ku FY2025; item 7's terrain source was wrong twice over, since PLATEAU ships its own 2.5 m TIN in the same archive and the right GSI layer would be the 1 m tile API; item 10 assumed a converter that has no Windows CLI; and **item 15 rested on a false premise** — parsed independently here, **1,678 of 1,741 AOI buildings (96.4%) are textured LOD2**, with a perfectly clean split, so procedural facades became the treatment for the untextured 3.6% and item 35 was appended for de-lighting the baked daylight out of the rest. Item 23 gained an ODbL 4.6 constraint: the OSM-derived graph stays in files of its own. **Conversion path decided empirically**: two `.b3dm` tiles from MLIT's own 3D Tiles build were parsed against the CityGML and win on per-building attributes (a 63-key batch table cross-checking exactly on Hikarie 173.6 m/34 and Shibuya Stream 171.3 m/35) and on addressability (`_BATCHID` plus a five-level hierarchy, 67 tiles over the AOI), so the untested Windows Rust build was not taken. **Two new gates, both made to go red**: `test/aoi.test.ts` caught a transposed EPSG:6677 axis pair, `test/elevation.test.ts` caught both a 36.877 m geoid slip and a Terrain-RGB misdecode. The attribution surface is in the running app and the blank-frame luminance spread it governs was re-measured at 2.20 against a floor of 6. All five gates green; all twelve frames looked at again at 1280x720.
