## 2. The open items that matter most

1. **The signature shot is unmet, and the cause is routing, not rendering.** The populated crossing pose is 14 of 14 frames byte-identical across 26.15 simulated seconds of the pedestrian green with zero pedestrians and zero vehicles in frame — a true depiction (3,000 rendered, 0 within 60 m, crossing events accumulating elsewhere), traced to `MAXIMUM_ROUTE_LENGTH_M` 700 m against a 505 m nearest portal and a >1,000 m round trip. The crossing change's after-arm at the acceptance population is still the measurement that would say whether routing them in fixes it without jamming again. Everything the plan records about the queueing residual stands.
2. **The flythrough's crowd leg does not serve its purpose, for a structural reason worth understanding before re-shooting.** The orbit target's height is pinned at the app's origin datum (15.200 m) while the knot's local ground is 26.36 m, so the view axis is 34.7° down and the whole frame is pavement; no input flattens it because panning is horizontal by construction. The subject never resolves (two near-black clumps, no limb at 6×, knots shifting zero pixels over 0.4 s — the queueing problem at pixel level). A re-shoot needs a low-ground anchor or a rig whose target height can move; the report at `artifacts/inspection-flythrough/REPORT-crowd.md` carries the arithmetic.
3. **The dusk criterion's flicker half is unanswerable from the passing run.** Its captures are 0.65–1.98 s and 39–119 rendered frames apart and TAA samples are 0 in every populated frame, so no lane could read crawl from them; the crowd lane can exclude crawl only while the camera is held (918,000+ of 921,600 pixels byte-identical across five frames). TAA itself is evidenced where it can run: all eight agent-free hero frames of the certificate record `taaAccumulating: true` (25–32 samples). Closing this criterion needs a consecutive-frame capture of a moving camera.
4. **Performance as written is still unmet, and the vehicle half is the owner's recorded infeasibility.** 59 of 200 vehicles were drawn in the acceptance arm; `holdsSixtyFps` reads false in every arm because 16.7 > 16.667 under the 100 µs clock clamp, which is the vsync reading rather than a miss; and the measured build exists nowhere on `main` because of the probe seam. The pedestrian half is now reported as met at the median with no margin left — it would not hold on a slower machine, and the p95 tail is one frame.
5. **The approach leg's medium materials finding is the most substantive new appearance defect.** `terrain.ts` and `roads.ts` use surface materials with no texture map, the ground's only albedo variation is a ±3% sinusoid at about 20 m, and the road's grain fades out above 0.015–0.08 m/px, so five of twelve approach frames are mostly a blank mid-ground surface (mid-ground sd 1.8–3.4 against 10.15 on a roof deck at a similar mean). It is not a hole or a NaN; it is the surface treatment at that distance. Fixing it changes appearance and needs a re-capture.
6. **Two plan sentences are now known to be false and are corrected in the plan**: the flight is not a closed loop (`ascent-010` measures 857.4 m against the leg's 620 m aim, `overview-000` 159 m short of 543 m, because per-step corrections are capped), and the ascent produces no exposure change (exposure constant at the dusk preset's value in all 22 frames).
7. **The clearance check's own bound**: it reads only captured instants. The approach leg's stand records show the camera 0.194 m above ground at step 9 and 1.078 m at step 10, captured at 2.685 m and 3.101 m — the path dips inside the 1.5 m threshold between frames and the check cannot see it. Recorded, not fixed.
