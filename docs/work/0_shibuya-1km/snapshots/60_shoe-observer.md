## Implemented contract

`tools/contact/finite-shoe-contact.ts` exports `commitPlant` and `observeShoe`. It is a reusable, synchronous finite-pose observer with no runtime dependencies. Its input is an explicitly bound actual shoe reference, complete posed vertex map, actual foot-joint load point, measured packing error, explicitly selected support triangles, optional height-field obstruction triangles and optional committed plant baseline. The adapter validates and hashes the actual source bytes before constructing these typed inputs; matching metadata inside this pure geometry function is not a substitute for that loader check. Support IDs are supplied, never chosen by height or proximity. A cap or different mesh identity is rejected as contact support.

Actual sole triangle geometry is normally projected onto each named support triangle and clipped inside that triangle and the strict 15 mm band. Each patch retains its shoe/support triangle IDs, original shoe points, normal projections, signed normal and vertical residuals, projected area, area uncertainty and explicit interior witness. A resolved positive-area contact hull must contain the vertical projection of the declared actual foot joint. Required opposed banks must each contribute a resolved positive-area patch. Contact-band eligibility and nonpenetration are separate verdicts. The 15 mm bound does not excuse sinking.

The whole shoe is checked against every supplied height-field support and obstruction by XZ triangle intersection and signed-height extrema. Degenerate projected shoe faces use clipped edge extrema, so a vertical shoe face cannot disappear from the check. A vertical or degenerate surface is explicitly unresolved: this implementation does not claim general vertical-wall/coplanar three-dimensional obstacle handling. Supplied obstruction surfaces have the same upward height-field, solid-below interpretation as ground; an arbitrary overhead sheet must not be passed as such an obstruction. These bounds are material before any city application.

`commitPlant` copies and freezes every selected sample ID and its world point. Later observations compare Euclidean displacement to that same baseline even when support triangles change. Sample membership changes are rejected. The bound is strictly below 5 mm in world metres, independent of actor scale. An omitted plant baseline means contact-only observation; `drift` is then null, not a passing planted-foot result. The caller still owns real plant/release events and must keep the same baseline until release. This instrument does not infer lifecycle or phase continuity.

Measured Float32 error and a separate arithmetic guard determine uncertain residual signs, threshold margins and near-zero areas. Contact hulls use resolved patches. Uncovered sole pieces are retained per original shoe triangle, without filling slits or holes. Their areas are triangle-piece sums, not a global mesh-union measurement. Tiny arithmetic residues on a fully covering plane are retained and labelled uncertain rather than reported as resolved holes.
