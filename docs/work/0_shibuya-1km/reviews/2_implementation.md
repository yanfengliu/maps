## Reports

### Independent asset reviewer

**A0 — P1: walk/idle blending moves planted feet at stops and low speed.** In `src/agents/render/humans.ts:141`, walk weight is the current speed divided by 0.2, capped at one. `src/agents/render/vat.ts` directly mixes that walk pose with an independently phased idle pose. There is no stance transition or planted-foot constraint. A slot at walk phase zero, idle phase zero and fixed actor origin moves its near-LOD sole 0.275146 m when speed changes from at least 0.2 m/s to zero. Reading the delivered half-float samples produced this displacement for all three variants. This is a position change, not a timing or shadow inference.

The same defect occurs during continuous slow movement. At a constant 0.1 m/s, walk weight is 0.5. Over sampled walk frames 0 through 15, the actor advances 0.515625 m in 5.15625 seconds. The mixed sole drifts 0.257874 m for both male variants and 0.257904 m for the female variant after actor movement is included. The idle samples also land exactly on frames zero and one, so this control does not approximate shader interpolation. The existing delivered-VAT gate measures the full walk clip and cannot see this runtime blend. Preserve contact through start, stop and low-speed transitions while keeping simulation position and travelled distance authoritative. Gate the composed runtime pose over transition phases and slow speeds, then inspect the resulting motion and all rendering passes.

**A1 — P2: the contact verifier accepts geometry outside the selected scene, and the renderer counts absent humans.** `tools/agents/verify.ts:48` visits every entry in `gltf.meshes`. It does not traverse the selected scene's nodes to establish which meshes the loader will instantiate. The reviewer changed only `scenes[scene].nodes` from `[59]` to `[]` in `commuter-male-near.glb`, rewrote the GLB length and JSON chunk correctly, and updated the manifest hash to `6eb0d0dd790a2c683753f483c74791c08c1bec1ef03ae077ef1a0dab65486a65`. All human verification still exited zero, including a claim of 22,322 glTF seam vertices and valid near contact. The selected scene contains no nodes.

A second probe passed empty selected scenes from all nine actual human GLBs through the installed GLTFLoader and current HumanRenderer. Loading succeeded, the group contained zero meshes, and `renderedCount` became three for three active slots. Thus the gap reaches runtime reporting as well as the offline gate. Enumerate mesh membership from the selected scene using the same VAT frame convention as the renderer. Reject an empty or incomplete required human draw set at admission, and report a rendered actor only when its selected LOD has the required drawable parts. Keep a valid-hash empty-scene or orphan-sole mutation as a negative control; a hash mismatch alone would test a different failure.

The numerical and runtime probes are retained under ignored `artifacts/agents/review2/`: `probe.mjs`, `probe-result.json`, `runtime.test.ts` and `vitest.config.ts`. The generated mutation directory was removed in the probe's `finally` cleanup; its construction and exact changed GLB digest remain reproducible from the frozen inputs.

**Known integration limits, separate from A0 and A1.** The current shared poses have XYZ, yaw and uniform scale only. `writePoseMatrix` supplies no pitch, roll or support normal, so a vehicle's front and rear wheel contact heights remain equal in world Y. A bus with the manifest's 6.136 m axle spacing would encounter a 0.3068 m front-to-rear support-height difference on a synthetic 5% grade. One scalar origin height cannot align both axles. This arithmetic describes the contract limit, not a measured Dogen-zaka grade. The root has identified support-plane alignment as a pending core/renderer integration contract. Pedestrian contact likewise needs the completed pavement surface; flat source VAT contact does not establish city foot height. Neither issue was reconstructed or repaired in this review.
