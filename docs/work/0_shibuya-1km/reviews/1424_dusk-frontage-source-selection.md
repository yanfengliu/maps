## Small implementation contract

Use one source-bound housing/lens treatment across the three walls, with restrained variation only when the actual wall arrangement requires it. Initial suggested body bounds are0.6m wide ×0.18m high, shallow enough to read as a wall fitting, with an opaque back/housing and a downward recessed lens. This is authored presentation, not a claimed reconstruction of photographed street hardware. Fit its entire mounting footprint to original source triangles and expose its physical contact. Do not erase or cover a photographed sign or opening; inspect the exact source UV field before choosing final placement. No source atlas semantics are admitted by this report.

For the east prototype, allow one short-range non-shadow downward SpotLight, or the existing local-light idiom if it can keep the response confined. Freeze one conservative initial colour/intensity/range before the comparison; do not retune environment/exposure/bloom or the four existing crossing fills. A proposed upper trial bound is60cd/range6m, with a receiver-directed cone and daylight power0. These are presentation limits for a visible trial, not measured cost or approved final values. The cone target must come from actual nearby supported receiver geometry, not an arbitrary Y offset. Inspect source side, back, nearby solid walls and roof to reject leakage. A non-shadow light cannot itself prove occlusion.

Keep the full set at three local lights, at most six beauty draws, at most1,500 authored triangles and128KiB of owned geometry, with no new image texture or atlas upgrade. These are proposed ceilings, not measured counts. Do not increase counts to rescue an invisible pilot. Each dynamic light adds shader work; three unshadowed lights can still cost more than their local visible footprint suggests. Keep existing geographic texture caps,640/768MiB cache policy, source textures and rail/building inputs unchanged. Do not add a universal window shader, glow decal, random occupancy grid or general support engine.

Likely implementation homes are a small source recipe and constructor beside `src/scene/commercial-corner.ts`, called from `src/scene/facade-textures.ts` before tile accounting, using its existing style/signage uniform options. Reuse that ownership pattern without expanding its corner-specific source cut or replacing peripheral photographs. Existing `src/scene/signage.ts` owns time/signage state and the separately authored boards/four crossing fills; `src/scene/lighting.ts` owns sun/hemisphere and needs no global correction for this task. A new runtime registry would broaden scope unnecessarily unless the existing uniform options prove insufficient.
