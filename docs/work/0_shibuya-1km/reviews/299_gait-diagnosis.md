## Diagnosis

The idle sample in `local-walk-rig.ts` is the immutable reference term for skinning, not a frozen final pose. Joint names/parents match the branches. There is no evidence here of a rig-axis inversion or missing upper-body binding.

The lower body does retain idle-reference hip locations and foot spacing. Every walking landing uses `f.home[0]`; the feet are ±0.156734 model metres from centre, versus hips ±0.088801. At the retained actor scale 1.0391258, ankle separation is 325.7 mm. Swing height is 35 model mm, or 36.4 mm displayed. This explains the broad stance and limited knee/foot lift without invoking a broken animation asset. The existing source walk has 105 mm authored lift, but its lower-body animation is deliberately replaced by contact IK.

The cached upper-body clip already supplies opposing arm swing. Direct inspection of its retained 32 matrices, aligned to the reference pelvis as the runtime does, gives left-wrist fore/aft range 235.4 mm, vertical range 23.6 mm and lateral range 0.83 mm in model space. The spine pose itself is effectively fixed relative to the pelvis; pelvis alignment removes the baked root bob. Adding another upper-body animation loader would duplicate an existing path. Source phase uses its own approximately 0.594 s clock at cruising speed and is not explicitly locked to actual alternating foot departures; that is a possible coordination limitation, not a demonstrated cause of the held frontal appearance. Leave that clock/clip unchanged in the first correction.

The camera and sampling amplify the limitation. Near views are only 2.90–4.11 degrees from the actor's forward axis; medium 3939/4160 are 111.80/115.79 degrees and expose fore/aft articulation. Near sampled times are 1.0017,1.3043,1.6034,2.2035,2.6037,3.0047s. Against recorded 0.297 s flights,1072 is 0.6% into swing;1122 is between flights;1173 is 96.6%;1270 is 85.4%;1329 is 6.9%;1394 is 35.3%. Thus five of six samples favour transfers or their vicinity. This is sampling bias, not proof that better images alone cure the gait. Recorded foot flights at cruising speed span roughly 0.72 m horizontally; a tiny forward stride is not the cause. Keep the existing near camera for the correction review.
