## Fundamental mistakes

We have repeatedly treated a small rendering treatment as a finished architectural idea. A photograph plus another grid retains the photograph's softness and adds a second competing window system. A brighter flat pane does not establish an occupied room. Reviews 701, 795 and 949 record those failures; this recommendation does not reopen their sources or propose another coefficient search. The next candidate must carry actual architectural content and coherent terminations, rather than a higher-contrast layer over the same unresolved façade.

The local cameras are also less exploratory than their labels suggest. The fixed close and block views share a bearing; seed42 changes azimuth by about 6.5 degrees and mainly moves distance/elevation. All six are steep aerial views of the same canyon. They are useful comparisons, but they omit a substantially different face and a lower view that could expose frontage depth, entrances or floating details. This is a sampling limit, not a defect in the accepted road increment.
