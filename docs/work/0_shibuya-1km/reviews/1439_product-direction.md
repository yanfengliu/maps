**Decision: continue adoption of Ground1437, then redirect from small material/window additions to a coherent road marking coverage milestone.** The ground grain and paving edges are legible in the close view. They merit preserving. Another subtle roughness adjustment or another small set of facade windows would have less benefit per delivery cycle than making the surrounding road network read as streets.

The three originals show the same imbalance at different scales. In plaza-az240, paving and crossing stripes are crisp while large roadway areas between the stripes remain visually undivided. In the noon approach, long plain dark road slabs beside and across the rail corridor remain conspicuous against the detailed track geometry. This is an observed presentation weakness in these views, not proof that every blank surface should carry paint or that a particular marking is missing in reality.

**One next outcome: bring the plan's 32 untreated road objects to a source-supported, visibly coherent marking treatment across the existing square.** Resolve their actual road/layer/source identities, then render the eligible published or reference-supported lane, edge and stop markings through the existing production paint path. Preserve legitimate unmarked roads. The milestone should improve connected approaches and junctions beyond the already treated crossing, including peripheral coverage, rather than selecting another isolated short edge for an attractive crop. Keep the five omitted urban-expressway carriageways as a separate elevated-layer dependency; this increment must not imply their completion.

This moves A5/item17 and the full-map part of A1/A14 closer to completion. Its breadth is more useful now than expanding the window count one facade at a time. The current marking provenance records 773 dropped unsupported polygons; that is not 773 recoverable defects, nor evidence that those polygons belong to the 32 objects. Some blank roads may have no usable paint source. Do not paint movement-graph centerlines, infer lane counts from width, bridge real source gaps, lift paint onto a different level, or relax the existing support limits to satisfy the milestone. Bind the selected roads to available source before expanding implementation. If current source cannot substantiate a coherent visible improvement, report this direction blocked by source coverage and stop; more asphalt noise cannot solve it.
