# 1436 — Satellite product direction

Date: 2026-10-17. Author: fresh-context product critic. Read-only assessment of three supplied production originals; no runtime, source, test, gate, browser or server work. Instructions, local rules and the current plan within its 12 KiB bound were read. Product Design audit guidance was applied to the explicitly supplied images; saved Product Design context was absent.

## Decision

Continue the Satellite direction and finish 1434 adoption subject to 1435's separate correctness/appearance verdict. The physical windows are legible in the block view and support the desired material depth. For the next product milestone, prioritize **shared asphalt and pavement material depth across the existing supported scene**. This has broader visible benefit than extending the current window treatment to another small set of buildings. This recommendation does not approve 1434 or close any of the 14 final criteria.

## Rendered evidence

1. **Block, azimuth 120:** the foreground window band reads as repeated framed openings, while surrounding buildings still rely heavily on soft photographs. The skyline and rail establish a coherent place. This image supports retaining the window work; it does not show that more windows are now the largest improvement.
2. **Dusk crossing hero:** the crossing layout, signs and lit ground-floor frontage make Shibuya recognizable. Broad smooth road sheets and a large flush paving grid dominate the lower half. Light pools supply most surface variation. Road, paint and paving have little convincing small-scale material separation at this distance.
3. **Plaza, azimuth 240:** the same weak ground response recurs on a different side of the crossing. Foreground paving reads as a regular flat grid; asphalt appears as smooth dark shapes around crisp markings. Photographic facade blur remains a substantial limitation, but this second close view makes ground a recurring, high-area opportunity rather than one isolated object.

These are scene viewpoints, not sequential interaction steps. Interface legibility, keyboard behavior, accessibility, motion, actor contact, whole-AOI appearance and continuous stability were not assessed.

## One next milestone

**Outcome:** existing Satellite streets and plazas read as materially distinct ground when moving around the crossing and its approaches. Give asphalt world-scale aggregate/detail and restrained roughness variation; give paving readable joint relief and modest tile variation. Treat wetness consistently with the existing dusk lighting. Preserve the current mapped layout, supported geometry, markings, tactile paths and established lighting/signage.

**Why now:** two independent close viewpoints devote much of their visible area to these surfaces. Improving the shared materials should carry through many roads and plazas in the square and improve the setting around the newly built frontage. The present evidence supports a material milestone; it does not justify more geometry, another data rebuild or simulation research.

**Bounded implementation contract:** work through the existing production ground-material paths on current supported road/pavement meshes. Use deterministic world-scale detail and existing material hooks. Keep source/cache bytes, support authority, paint placement, texture geographic policy and scene layout fixed. Retain shared geometry and basic style continuity. No new source, general material framework, union machinery, launch/report layer or new review instrument. Record the actual material paths and affected surface coverage before implementation; feasibility is not established by this critique.

**Acceptance:** at native 1280×720, the two named close views must show visibly improved asphalt/paving separation, joints and surface response without relying on extra color or unreadable micro-noise. Detail must hold at both street and block distances, with restrained highlights, readable paint and no obvious tiling, shimmer or source seams. Use the existing ordinary app, real controls, noon/dusk views and a held-out approach to check that the benefit carries beyond these two frames. Preserve the adopted facade, tactile and lighting work. Obtain independent review and the unchanged five public gates on the final integrated revision. This is a bounded material increment, not full-map or temporal acceptance.

**Uncertainties and disqualifier:** these stills cannot separate the contribution of material parameters from exposure, lighting and filtering, establish the current shader cost, or show every material path. If a bounded comparison through the actual production path yields only a tint/gloss change, detail invisible at the intended distance, or needs source changes/new geometry to look convincing, redirect before expanding the implementation. A coherent visible improvement across representative ground surfaces is the done condition; a new shader or extra noise alone is not.
