## Different observation design: conservative uncertainty admission

Instead of assuming observer cost cancels between arms, preserve each actual180-row render envelope and require an independently established per-row perturbation bound U. For each arm and row, the prerequisite is `abs(observed envelope - equivalent uninstrumented envelope) <= U`. The equivalent envelope means the same commands, actual source/scene/actor/post/pose/hardware and query boundaries. A CPU hook duration is not automatically U: scheduling, queue gaps, cache behavior and GPU frequency can make perturbation differ from direct CPU work.

With that prerequisite proved, mean monotonicity and nearest-rank quantile monotonicity give a scoreable conservative test for each existing ABBA pair and pose:

- Upper added mean = observed candidate mean minus observed baseline mean, plus mean(U candidate) plus mean(U baseline); require<=1 ms.
- Upper added p95 = observed candidate p95 minus observed baseline p95, plus max(U candidate) plus max(U baseline); require<=2 ms.

This preserves the original product cost bounds without subtraction, divided poll cost, assumed cancellation, pooled pairs or dropped rows. Each U must include all coupled render/drain/completion work. Retain every drain/completion as its own undivided resource interval, with all original checks; the mean of measured-row U keeps its180-row denominator and cannot be diluted with extra intervals. No small uncertainty p95 can replace its maximum in the quantile inequality. Startup/control/canonical hardware/seeded-pixel checks and full17-window accounting remain intact. The old0.25 guard remains binding until an independently reviewed requirement-preserving correction explicitly changes the instrument; this mathematical design is not that adopted correction.

**Exact gap:** neither raw908 nor the current instrumentation establishes U. Measuring or deriving a trustworthy complete causal perturbation bound without omitting the required work is the original hard part, now stated explicitly.
