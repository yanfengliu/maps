## Findings

### RD1 — High: direct terrain draping produces visibly implausible outer tracks

The proposal at source `REPORT.md:40` permits rails and narrow beds to conform to existing terrain. The app exposes that surface directly through `src/scene/terrain.ts:84`, using the barycentric sampler at `src/world/surface-sampler.ts:4`. Sampling the proposed centre lines every 0.5 m in world Z finds no missing terrain, but presence is not suitable rail support.

For way 146060273, the centre rises from Y 19.111599 at `(67.679592,-122.5)` to Y 19.461436 at `(67.641541,-122)`: 0.697657 rise/run over that interval. At Z -73 its centre is X 63.510542; terrain at X ±0.5335 is Y 19.112720 and 19.934310, a **0.821590 m difference across 1.067 m**. For way 155269603, Z -100 gives Y 20.049744 and 19.376674 across the same offsets: **0.673071 m difference**. That track also has a sampled 0.183642 longitudinal rise/run near Z -101. The two inner lines have much smaller sampled maxima, about 0.0350 and 0.0371 rise/run.

A small constant lift cannot remove these kinks or cross-track slopes. Draping both rails separately twists the track; lifting an entire sleeper from its centre leaves its ends intersecting or floating. Reject the direct-drape rule. Use a deliberately authored, continuous rail profile and cross-section with an explicit supporting bed/shoulder wherever it departs from the terrain. Preserve source X/Z and disclose the authored Y. Do not globally regrade terrain, move source lines to the plateau, or silently omit the difficult outer tracks.

### RD2 — High: the proposed interior still crosses existing road geometry

Source `REPORT.md:42` excludes the known south bridge ways, but this does not make the remaining interior conflict-free. All four proposed track strips meet `roads.mesh` near **Z -99.5 through -92.5**. At Z -96 the centre of way 146060426 has terrain Y 20.107772 and road Y 20.295408; way 155269600 has terrain Y 20.056312 and road Y 20.266284. These are about 0.19–0.21 m apart, within the ordinary presentation layers. A rail lift or ballast texture over this location would create a road/rail collision or conceal the road, violating the explicit no-overlap contract at source `REPORT.md:44`.

This is a rendered-surface conflict, not proof that the real place has a level crossing. `src/scene/roads.ts:16-23` documents that many PLATEAU road surfaces are draped, so this road's current Y must not be promoted into real road/rail topology. Before choosing a bed profile, identify the crossing's actual road triangles and whether the scene represents an underpass, overpass or source drape artifact. A small support design must name the crossing, the kept surface, any separately authorized local correction, and the resulting visible clearance. Do not solve it by painting through asphalt, adding a 0.3 m lift, discarding arbitrary rail fragments, or raising the whole corridor enough to clear an unknown structure.

### RD3 — Medium: arbitrary ends remain a visible design defect, not merely a scope disclaimer

The Z -230 and -30 limits at source `REPORT.md:38-42` cut four continuing railways in open corridor space. They stop roughly 45 m before the full north source ends and 18 m before the known south bridge transition. The inspected block images show a continuous, exposed strip leading toward the station. Four aligned ballast/rail ends within that strip risk reading as four short disconnected model tracks. A text disclaimer does not fix their appearance, and fading the tracks out would invent a different discontinuity.

The interior clip is acceptable as an ignored feasibility prototype. It is not a delivery boundary until endpoint views establish a coherent termination at a real occluder, source feature transition with a treated continuation, or the outer scene boundary. If neither endpoint can meet that condition, enlarge the static scenery increment to the nearest coherent boundaries and review its added support. Ending at an OSM way node alone is insufficient: a way split need not be a physical end. The untouched near witness remains unresolved even if the middle and far witnesses improve.
