The predeclared radiometric prediction is specific: at an independently attributed upper wall pixel, removing emission removes the source RGB above, multiplied by that pane's1-R and the actual post response. At a ceiling pixel the much larger ceiling vector is removed. If those changes do not appear despite a valid full-history intervention, the assumed room contribution or its attribution is wrong or another draw masks it; stop energy tuning there. If the measured changes follow that prediction on rear, return, floor and ceiling pixels, the actual current energy coupling is established and an opening-aware replacement can be evaluated on its own merits. It still must make connected enclosed depth readable across all eight views. An unchanged native failure or a merely brighter field rejects it.
