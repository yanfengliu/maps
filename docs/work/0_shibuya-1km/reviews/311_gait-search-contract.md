## Disqualifiers

- Translation or alternate feet alone do not prove natural walking.
- Do not exaggerate stylized motion to suit one camera.
- Do not change camera or phase solely to hide defects.
- Do not relax contact, root or clock tolerances.
- Static images do not prove continuous motion.
- Do not accept unplayed footage.
- Do not exclude errors or retry until clean.
- Do not change default startup, controller history or the portable installer.
- Do not tune on one camera without holding out the other profile.
