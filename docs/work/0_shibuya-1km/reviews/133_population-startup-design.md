## Decision

Reject long pre-roll as the product startup path. Do not run an extended warmup pilot. The already measured 5,463-step prefix takes 34.516 seconds of CPU and still leaves every pedestrian beyond 407.94 m and every vehicle beyond 426.08 m. A longer run contains that same empty prefix; its eventual vehicle route and contact outcomes are unproved. Backgrounding the work or hiding it behind asset loading does not remove the cost. This is a product decision from observed cost, not a claimed benchmark of every browser or machine.

Recommend one constructor-only pedestrian prototype: at most 24 initial pedestrians drawn from the existing 3,000 slots, with an all-or-nothing 24-person allocation under the checks below. The remaining 2,976 slots use the existing boundary startup. Initial people have real directed plans and use ordinary admission on their first and every later crossing. After their first retirement, their slots also use ordinary boundary replenishment. Twenty-four is a proposed small visible-group target, not a measured packing capacity; failure to place all 24 is a refusal, not permission to reduce the target silently.

Vehicle initialization depends on a legal route witness that does not yet exist. Do not implement an interior vehicle activation shortcut while that dependency is red. A separate finite investigation may explain or repair the current planner's refusal using existing directed edges, actual body dimensions and the unchanged passage factory. Raw connectivity, a different random seed, a larger route budget, an invented exit or a shortened body is not the witness. The accepted 200-vehicle infeasibility exception stays accepted.

## Contracts the prototype must preserve

**Construction boundary.** Introduce an explicit initial-population policy owned by population construction, separate from normal `planSlot`. The prototype receives immutable current network, asset and displayed-surface inputs; it returns either a complete initial allocation or named refusals. It runs once, before fixed tick 1 and before any renderer reads active buffers. It cannot be called after stepping, on style changes, after a slot retires, or by the read-only harness. No checkpoint, persisted world, renderer writer or second clock is needed.

**Slots, routes and randomness.** Reserve exactly slots 0–23 of the requested pedestrian capacity for the successful initial allocation; they remain part of the 3,000, not extra render actors. Use the existing scene seed, generation/slot RNG, actual selected variant and scale, and `RouteLibrary`/real passage factory. A candidate is a complete directed route beginning on a supported interior sidewalk and ending on the existing body-clear pedestrian terminus. Its route starts at zero; it does not pretend to have travelled an earlier boundary prefix. Every passage, gate, mapped control and occurrence index must describe that actual complete route. A supplied edge ID or hand-built passage is not trusted. Do not seed on crossings or inside any physical conflict primitive, even when `junctionId` is null. `footprintOccupies` evaluates compound physical areas, not merely the compound's large display radius (`footprints.ts:42–48`).

**Finite allocation.** Enumerate the same existing null-junction starts whose first point is within 150 m, stable-sorted by ID, with a hard maximum of 512 candidate starts. Attempt at most one current seeded route per candidate/slot with the existing three-attempt/120-edge limits; no new random seed or changed cap after a refusal. Consider at most 24 slots, for at most 12,288 candidate/slot checks. Actual selected pose, lateral corridor offset, variant and scale determine admission; the max-scale diagnostic above is not reused as a runtime permit. Record each candidate refusal and selected route/pose. Greedy allocation is acceptable and may fail; it must not claim that failure proves no packing exists.

**Footprint, spacing and initial support.** Before publishing any initial active byte, validate the actual displayed initial body transforms, their full collision footprints and the complete batch. Every footprint must be outside every physical conflict area; every pair must be disjoint in world space, including nearby starts from distinct or reversing routes. Require at least the sum of actual collision radii plus the existing 0.05 m gate margin as centre separation for pedestrians; use the actual polygons as an additional nonintersection check. Include already prepared/active actors if initialization is ever generalized, although this prototype constructs the initial batch before boundary population exists. No origin-only clearance, same-route-only test or numerical nudge is allowed.

Initial support needs current byte-bound displayed road/pavement/source-ground triangles, route/source identity, and a selected unambiguous physical level. A network edge's interpolated Y, `junctionId: null`, ground ray hit, terrain fallback or corridor width does not establish support. Check complete collision-footprint coverage and the actual initial rendered foot/base placement against that same surface; use the existing 15 mm contact limit, without raising floors or moving route points. Reject gaps, wrong levels and uncertainty that prevents the bound being established. Reuse an applicable, correctly bound existing query; do not call the held mixed-reference F37 adapter or infer that a source-reference check passed from matching labels. This is a bounded static initial-pose check, not a replacement for Q1's moving finite-shoe, 5 mm drift or source-reference obligations. If a valid local support witness is unavailable, report it and keep the constructor result unadmitted; do not reopen whole-city contact research as a prerequisite for authoring this small probe.

**Initial poses and time.** Construct both current and previous pose halves from the same admitted pose, yaw, support normal, scale, variant and fresh generation. Set travelled distance and speed to zero, stopped/queued duration to zero, committed false, and no held lease or inherited request age. Initialize the crowd's matching state too. `spawnSlot` changes generations/active bytes but does not copy positions (`slots.ts:207–214`), so calling it alone does not establish first-frame interpolation correctness. Stage the complete initial pose before making the population visible. Keep the same `RenderLoop` and `JunctionAdmissions`; initial simulation time is zero. Preserve the asset-loading hold and release only after the real renderer mounts (`app.ts:185–196,278–300`). Loading latency must not choose the world age.

**Admissions and lifecycle.** From fixed tick 1 use the unchanged lifecycle → plan → request → resolve → integrate → close sequence (`tick.ts:1–25,58`). Initial actors receive no grant, fabricated dwell, reservation, artificial crossing count or signal offset. The ordinary first request must arise from the actual approach and footprint. Count initialized occupants separately in diagnostic status while including them once in the existing spawned/active conservation identity. They must not inflate boundary-spawn or completed-route counters. Keep `pedestrian:<slot>` identity and the existing fresh-generation semantics. Existing body-clear terminal release/retirement applies to their real route; after retirement the next generation is planned only at an ordinary boundary portal. No repeated interior reappearance is allowed.

**Vehicles, when a witness exists.** The current boundary API deliberately refuses interior activation (`admissions.ts:143`). Do not relax that check or call boundary activation after temporarily posing a car at a false portal. `bindBoundaryActor` already validates directed route and a true AOI exit, retaining slot/generation/pose identity (`admissions.ts:131–140`); construction-only initial activation would need its own reviewed operation with all checks before mutation. It must reject any pre-existing commitment, touched physical conflict, unsupported body/wheel pose, duplicate/stale binding or call after the initial construction phase. It grants no passage. The same binding must later serve ordinary observation and unchanged whole-body boundary egress/retirement (`admissions.ts:175–194`). Vehicle initialization is not included in the proposed 24-person prototype.

## Finite next prototype and acceptance

Root may authorize a pure constructor/initial-state prototype with no browser, no startup default change, no route-data edit and no production activation yet. Freeze the 24-person allocation policy above, current source/data/asset hashes and one default seed before running. A five-second wall cap for one constructor attempt is a proposed product budget for this machine, not a measured capability. Exceeding it fails the prototype; do not run a longer startup or silently take fewer people. The candidate/slot cap is an independent deterministic stopping condition.
