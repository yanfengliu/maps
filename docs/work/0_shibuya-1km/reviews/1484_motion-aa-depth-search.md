Hypothesis D1: road/pavement/terrain depth quantization, wrong ordering, transparent paint or clipping causes the apparent missing paint at this central checker.

Prediction: an allegedly missing painted sample has paint geometry whose depth competes with a receiver at about a few representable depth steps, or a clip/alpha/order state rejects it. Falsifier for that local explanation: paint is opaque, unclipped and thousands of steps in front, or no paint triangle exists at the selected geometric sample before any depth test.

Hypothesis D2: normal/depth pass loses material ownership. Source inspection finds withNormalMaterials preserves clipping planes but replaces ordinary road/pavement/paint materials with a single ordinary MeshNormalMaterial. Their(-2,-4)/(-3,-6) polygon offsets are therefore absent from GTAO's geometry pass. Its G-buffer is also separate from the current-frame sampled color. This is a real cross-pass state difference, but these twelve raw geometric cases do not need either offset to keep paint ahead of road. No rendered AO difference was observed in this branch, so changing normal-pass state is not yet a supported fix. Any later presentation-only variant must preserve source geometry, actual cutouts and animated/cutout normal materials; risks include tactile paint, published markings, thin facade glazing, culling and AO edge alignment.
