## Exact bounded candidate and discriminator

A coherent construction probe would retain all ground geometry/materials, threshold/rear joint, mounted identity signs, roof/advertisements and original source channels/strict placement guards. Only the seven authored upper storeys change. Use one continuous clipped interior strip per face and storey with the existing 2.25 m depth, sharing floor/ceiling/back construction across four old bays. Join the two strips through the interior corner and remove internal partition/bisector walls. Keep physical returns at the facade ends. Generate these plans against the actual displayed pentagon, replacing current upper room triangles instead of appending a second room system. Close the measured gaps; do not use the diagnostic wall-deletion filter as the builder.

Put the upper glazing surface 40 mm behind the facade and shorten existing upper frame returns to the same shallow construction. Preserve the present eight floor heights and the continuous corner join. Split each old quarter-face opening into four slender lites visually with 25 mm vertical metal blades; keep the current glass quad topology below them. There are three added blades per old bay,168 blades across seven storeys/two faces. Each blade needs only a front and two side quads, six triangles, because its ends terminate inside the existing horizontal frame. Do not keep the 420 mm-deep old frame beside a shallow new one. Keep partial blinds/objects behind the skin as actual clipped objects; their current sparse arrangement is not a facade texture. This probe adds no glazing wallpaper, duplicated source window grid, atlas, light, pass, environment or post change. Keep current upper physical-glass shader fields initially to discriminate construction from a material change.

Do not declare success from the reduced partition percentage or changed material name. A fresh native critic settles that visible distinction.

## Remaining gap and cost

The mechanism does not yet prove glass. Alpha-composited blue-grey panes can still look like air or opaque infill after construction changes. No reflection system is proposed or assumed as a missing future component. If the unchanged material still fails after this architectural discriminator, construction alone is insufficient under these assumptions; that is a result to record, not a request to move the hard part to an unbuilt reflection dependency.
