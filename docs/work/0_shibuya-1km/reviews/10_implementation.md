**F10 — [P2] Highest-surface paint projection joins a real crossing to an upper road layer.** `src/scene/roads.ts:82` returns the highest road or pavement height at an XY point. The app supplies that query, with a terrain fallback, to `createStreetDetails`. `SurfaceBuilder` independently samples every corner without the physical path's expected elevation or a coherent surface identity. At an overlapping road layer, adjacent paint corners can therefore select different physical surfaces.

The independent full source centreline census sampled 4,463 points from painted zebra crossings and path-shaped tactile features. A second probe calls the actual `createStreetDetails` on that unchanged source feature, including its 3 m inferred width and original path. It uses the same road/pavement/terrain query as the app. The renderer emits 12 stripes and 504 vertices.
