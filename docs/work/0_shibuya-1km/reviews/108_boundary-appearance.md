## Verdict

No blocking cutout appearance defect found in these 56 images. Preliminary appearance acceptance only. A fully bound supplemental replay remains required because this run did not record the build and served-input inventories before capture.

The 24 whole-footprint frames fit the complete model and all four corner regions with margin. The terrain outline reads as four straight boundaries where visible. Retained buildings can obscure the exact rear ground corners in projection; these views do not prove that each mathematical ground vertex is unobstructed. The 32 close frames expose all four corners and all four side midpoints in both styles at noon and dusk. The visible terrain walls are continuous, close cleanly at the corners, and end at a common straight base. Building cuts are filled neutral faces with restrained flat material, while photographic or procedural facade patterns remain on original exterior surfaces.

I found no visible detached apron, floating cut fragment, open building interior, roof hole at the shown cuts, hanging paint strip, or separated bright halo. No obvious detached AO or shadow residue is visible; static city images cannot establish the origin of every shaded region, so the independent diagnostic controls remain necessary.

## Concrete observations and limits

- O1 — Attached cap bottoms appear below the local terrain surface on several side views. The clearest example is `noon-cartographic-midpoint-north.png`, approximately x647–687/y320–337, with smaller examples along the south side. They remain attached to their cap and the terrain wall remains solid. This is not a floating sliver or an empty gap. These images alone do not establish whether the visible lower portions represent original source building bottoms; do not invent that explanation or silently remove source geometry from this observation.
- O2 — Shaded Satellite terrain sides are nearly black at noon and dusk, while lit faces carry muted olive or warm brown. Cartographic sections remain more legible. The strong contrast is visually heavy in close views but still reads as a solid matte model, and does not rise to a blocking closure or appearance defect against the approved section design.
- O3 — Close Satellite imagery shows blurred facade photography, especially the central large facade in the south midpoint, and local uneven terrain around the southeast street. These are visible quality limits. A still candidate-only review cannot attribute them to this change; no new cut-specific cavity or geometry detachment was found there.
- O4 — Thin light pixels follow some Cartographic top edges. They are attached to the surface/cap boundary and do not form a detached halo or a raised rim that obscures the city.
- O5 — The close captures crop distant building tops and the UI covers some background city. Their intended corner or side segment remains visible. Whole-footprint captures provide the complementary overview. Neither set establishes the invisible underside or watertightness below the visible section.
