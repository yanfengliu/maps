## Reports

### Codex shoe_observer_review

**Reject this revision for diagnostic admission until F34 is repaired and independently re-reviewed.** The source/control evidence reproduces, but the uncertainty-aware contact hull can report a false pass. This is a defect in the declared finite geometry contract, not a demand for continuous physics or a larger model.

**F34 — A resolved patch's nominal clipped boundary is not a resolved support hull.** Lines 135–142 clip at the nominal 15 mm band and label the whole patch resolved when its area and one interior witness have sufficient margin. Lines 159–162 then use every nominal clipped boundary vertex in the load hull, with only a direct `epsilonM` hull-edge margin. Near-parallel shoe/support planes amplify a tiny normal error into a much larger movement of the band-clipped boundary. A center with safe contact margin establishes that some contact area remains; it does not establish that the old boundary still supports the load point.

Root supplied the initial almost-parallel hypothesis, and I constructed and executed the independent public-API reproduction. For `y(x) = 0.0149998 + 0.0000006*x` on a finite rectangle with `x` in `[0,1]`, packing error `5e-8` m and load `x = 0.30`, the observer returns aggregate `pass`. Its contact hull ends at `x = 0.3333333333314059`. Moving every vertex upward by exactly that allowed error yields contact that remains positive-area, but its hull ends at `x = 0.2499999999985544`; the load margin becomes `-0.050000000001445576` m and the exact displaced geometry fails. An interior load at `x = 0.20` passes and an outside-nominal load at `x = 0.40` fails, confirming that this is the hull boundary behavior rather than a permanently positive classifier.

A second reviewer-authored control measures the actual Float32 round trip instead of assuming an error vector. A 0.125 m by 0.125 m analytic rectangle has left-edge Y `0.0149999985` and right-edge Y `0.015000003`; X/Z are exactly representable. Rounding each vertex coordinate with `Math.fround` produces a measured maximum Euclidean discrepancy of `3.900141719997974e-10` m. The nominal pose with that measured error returns `pass`, with hull maximum X `0.04166666666666667`. At load X `0.0375`, its reported minimum margin is `0.00416666666024177` m. Evaluating the actual rounded vertices with zero remaining packing error returns `fail`: maximum X is `0.03399999998509884` and load margin is `-0.00350000001490116` m. Thus the uncertainty-bearing nominal result certifies support that the very packed geometry its error describes lacks. These are finite analytic controls, not a claim that any of the four actual-source poses failed.

The repair must derive hull support from contact geometry justified under the supplied uncertainty, or report the affected hull as uncertain. Preserve nominal boundary evidence separately where useful. Do not merely enlarge a hull margin by the raw packing length, bless the center as the whole patch, alter the 15 mm threshold, or require the existing positive contact controls to fail indiscriminately. Retain both analytic reproductions and positive/negative neighbors as regression controls; rerun the actual-source set and re-review the exact repair.
