## Reports

### Codex contact_design_review

**Recommend admission of the bounded diagnostic design with the execution-order clarifications below. Do not admit a working nine-case path or a controller extension from this report.** The proposal follows Review 40: select local surface ownership explicitly, fit support before measuring physical arc, observe actual displayed shoe geometry, retain source authority and geometric holds, and keep stationary turning separate from accepted locomotion. It preserves 15 mm contact and 5 mm planted drift, all 150 route identities, all nine named rows, original mesh gaps and the distinction between historical eligibility and current support.

The actual two gaps remain 0.12470795686449207 mm and 0.10880193704429904 mm. The author's independent chord calculation reproduces the earlier geometry within `9.076073226310655e-15` m. The seven exterior cases have inward corridor room, but no complete replacement route or actual candidate foot state exists. Origin hits at stations 28, 30 and 32 do not establish a supported prefix or a usable finite-foot endpoint. Several later preferred points miss the upper set, so choosing a convenient common exit at 44–50 m would invent support. These are reasons to construct and disclose provisional inputs, not proof of route impossibility.

**W2/W3 — Freeze geometry inputs before replay; validate the feet afterward.** The phrase “once supported endpoints have been supplied” must not require already validated actual planted feet before the causal replay that produces them. Root may admit a provisional geometry assignment, entry/exit and supported body-height construction as an explicit hypothesis. The replay then judges incoming/outgoing planted feet and reports a failed endpoint as a failed candidate. Root agreed to this ordering during the review. No source-policy authority follows from that agreement.

The assignment must cover the causal prefix from route birth, the local repair and the declared exit. Naming the upper feature only at the seam leaves the initial state and approach undefined. Conversely, requiring a fully accepted production correspondence before any diagnostic would exceed Review 40. A finite prefix may have explicitly selected intervals on other current sheets, with each transition retained for checking. An unresolved transition remains a refusal. Historical sampler/old-road-winner lineage is a possible follow-up source of justification; this review neither performs that trace nor adopts it as selection policy. Current nearest-Y, absent level tags and feature IDs alone remain insufficient.

Select the command schedule, initial phase convention, provisional endpoints and candidate order before inspecting contact results. Contact can reject a geometrically ranked candidate; it cannot choose a favorable phase or move the endpoint past a bad plant. Freeze the finite search bounds and record all attempted/rejected candidates. The first sixteen candidates are a search bound, and a failure within that bound says nothing about global feasibility. Missing inputs or a first-refusal stop stay visible among the same nine rows.

**W1/W3 — The rigid-shoe model is a useful, declared diagnostic bound.** Positive-area real contact patches whose hull contains the declared foot-joint load projection make the old minimum-height test less vacuous. Opposed-bank contacts can support an actual rigid shoe over an interior slit. The model need not require ground beneath every sole interior point. A wider hole may also pass this ideal rigid-shoe model if the actual shoe spans it with the required patches; that result would not establish anatomical or visual plausibility. Preserve the hole and its size in the output. A one-bank case whose hull misses the load point must fail, and a height step must retain both real heights rather than be replaced by an interpolated contact triangle.

Root resolved the residual metric left implicit in the authored prose: absolute normal distance to the named selected support triangle must be below 0.015 m; report both signed normal and signed vertical residuals. World-space Euclidean planted drift must remain below 0.005 m. This is an explicit extension of the earlier asset vertical check, not a claim that the new sloped-domain predicate has already passed. Nonpenetration stays separate; the 15 mm band never permits a shoe intersection. Each retained sample keeps its own committed plant baseline across triangle changes. No sample-set or baseline reset may hide slip. Root also agreed that a wider hole can pass the declared ideal rigid-shoe hull while its uncovered region remains reported, without anatomical acceptance or a hidden maximum-gap threshold. These decisions supplement the exact authored snapshot; its bytes remain unchanged.

The control manifest must state expected outcomes, not merely list shapes: narrow slit with opposed contacts passes the declared contact model; unsupported origin alone supplies no verdict; one-bank/load-point failure, wrong sheet, cap-only substitution, penetration, no witness within 15 mm and slip over 5 mm fail their respective predicates. A genuine hole control must prove the uncovered region was retained even where the ideal rigid-shoe hull permits bridging it. Near-zero patch area or penetration sign within the measured packing uncertainty is unresolved, not a silently positive contact. These conditions make the declared model testable without introducing a new anatomical area threshold or force simulation.

The actual selected LOD, scale, source palette and complete skin weights must stay together. Normalizing world coordinates and speed by one fixed actor scale is a viable unchanged-controller diagnostic; transforming returned geometry and anchors back to world metres must occur once. The 15/5 mm bounds are not scaled. The yaw-only diagnostic can expose slope failure but cannot inherit sloped-basis acceptance. Matching office-male and commuter-female reference inputs are needed for four rows. Their absence should not prevent the five commuter-male rows, including both seams, from producing explicitly partial evidence. No substitute rig or reduced denominator is acceptable.

**W4/F26/F27 — Observe the real update sequence before changing coupling.** F26 can translate, turn and become held during one fixed tick. Its aggregate final pose cannot reconstruct that history for F27. The proposed read-only ordered-segment observer is the smallest useful implementation increment: expose the actual already computed segments and held remainder; assert exact equality of all old return values with observation disabled and enabled; retain source-plateau, no-rewind, rotating-footprint and hold/release controls. The observer must emit nothing that changes arithmetic or authority. Each fixed tick's observed subintervals must be ordered, contiguous and sum to that tick's duration, including a zero-motion remainder.

The current F27 controller cannot be assumed to admit moving feet or hand back to walking. Independent deny-all controls depart at ticks 14, 7 and 5 for 0.3, 0.6 and 0.9 m/s respectively, with zero admission callbacks. An already aligned stationary-turn state also throws on the following positive-speed update because `turnControl` remains present. These reproduce the design's disclosed limitations. They lie outside Review 50's stationary-turn acceptance and do not reopen it.

An unchanged-controller replay can still provide diagnostic failure observations. It must not report successful coupling because geometry happened to pass after an unadmitted movement. The later moving-admission, explicit landing orientation, world-anchor and handback changes remain separately reviewed implementation work. Define the atomic unit before that coupling: stage the complete ordered fixed-tick body/controller result, and reject it together if a later subinterval is refused. Include a control that denies after an earlier segment of the same tick, plus landing/redeparture and held-turn cancellation controls. No reset, rewind of committed body state, fake travelled distance or discarded airborne time can repair the observation after the fact.

The smallest concrete work sequence is now clear. First implement and verify the F26 observer against existing analytic controls, and implement the finite shoe observer against the retained commuter-male reference and the declared synthetic contact controls. Neither needs a source decision or new variant extraction. In parallel, a bounded geometry worker can nominate explicit conditional prefix/local-boundary inputs from the frozen routes and current meshes, retaining competing sheets and unresolved transitions. Root freezes those inputs and one schedule before causal replay. Begin actual-case evidence with the two unchanged-XZ commuter-male seam rows; keep the other seven rows pending or refused with exact reasons. Expand to the inward candidate graph and the other variants only as their inputs exist. This sequence produces reviewable instruments and then a real attempted stance; it does not ask root to supply a prevalidated stance or wait for a new general architecture.
