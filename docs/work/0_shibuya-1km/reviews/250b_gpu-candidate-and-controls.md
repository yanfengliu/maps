The edge branch computes the difference of idle bases plus sum(D × difference-of-H), over the full endpoint joint union. This equals the difference of the position law in real arithmetic. With a positive finite affine W, dividing the matrix by W is equivalent to the original homogeneous application. It does not assume W equals one.
