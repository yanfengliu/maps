# Quality work register — the Shibuya 1km deliverable

Status: active, 2026-09-18. Owner: the lead (coordinator). This register is the prioritized list the quality goal asks for: what is fixed, what is prescribed and why it waits, and what is rejected. Its evidence home is the plan's dated status block; the audit that produced the ranking and the file:line verification is `artifacts/quality-audit/register.md` (ignored), summarized here so a reader of `main` sees it.

The organizing rule is the certificate: the 44 certified frames and their native inspection are the deliverable's headline evidence, so a change that moves `dist/index.html`, the bundle hashes, or `data/scene` strands that evidence and must be batched into a single re-capture rather than landed alone. Phase A is everything that does not; Phase B is the one batch that does; the signature shot's stall is prescribed with a separate capture of its own, below. Corrections below follow `docs/policies/local-rules.md`'s rule: a citation that says a change landed names a commit reachable from `main`. ## Rejected or deferred, with the reason

- **Satisfying the dusk criterion's TAA clause over a moving sequence.** The app cannot do it: `src/app.ts` feeds `cameraStill` into `post.setStill` and `post.ts` resets the sample index, so accumulation and motion are mutually exclusive by construction. The criterion has to be judged on the non-accumulating path, or the app's stillness policy has to change — which is a product decision, not a quality fix.
- **Landing F1 or the probe seam alone.** Superseded: the probe seam landed on `main` as `f8c9e7c`, and F1 is now the batch itself, landing there with the pavement-input review rather than alone. The reason the pair was rejected still holds for anything else that moves `dist/` or `data/scene`: it strands the certified frame set and the digest-bound inspection.
- **The signature shot's routing bound as the cause.** **Falsified 2026-09-18**, and its lever is closed rather than deferred. `MAXIMUM_ROUTE_LENGTH_M = 700` (`src/agents/population/routes.ts:85`) is read at one site, `routes.ts:528` inside the legacy `walk()`; a spawn asks for a centre route first and **falls back to `walk()` when it is refused** (`planPedestrianCentreRoute`, `tick.ts:486-490`, called at `tick.ts:638`; the centre shape's own cap is `MAXIMUM_CENTRE_ROUTE_LENGTH_M = 1_000`, `routes.ts:113`), so the 700 m cap still binds the portals whose centre plan is refused — **14 of the 31 drawn portals**, about **1,365 of 3,000 slots** (`artifacts/signature-routing/route-mix-p3000-t900-before.json` records 1,369 centre-route refusals across 14 portals) — but it is not the measured blocker: **1,533 of 3,000** slots already drive a crossing route, 18 of 33 portals reach a central crossing at 500.038–828.255 m with 10 inside the cap (`artifacts/signature-routing/centre-route-census.json`), and driving the crossing share 51.1% → 93.9% buys 1 → 3 diagonal entries while completions fall 338 → 2, overlapping pairs rise 142 → 324 and deepest penetration 0.4909 → 0.5137 m (`plan.md`'s status block). - **A light or palette lift for the road grain.** The wrong lever, and now measured as such: the display step grows sub-linearly with strength, so reaching the pavement control's share by amplitude alone needs 0.9–1.0 of the tile's strength, and the arm at 1.1 is the one whose near field reads as **oil-stained blotches** rather than aggregate (`artifacts/grain/arms.md` carries the arms).
