## The bound on this register

Every cost figure above is the audit's measurement, not an estimate, and every recapture dependency was verified in `vite.config.ts`, `index.html` or the digest algorithm rather than assumed. What the register does not claim: that the Phase B batch is free of risk — a materials change touches every frame and could move the hero look enough to invalidate the tone judgments of round 30 and this session's inspection, which is exactly why the batch is re-inspected rather than assumed.

**Two traps for a reader verifying the certificate against the frames, both found by the re-inspection.** First, `complete.json` keys its frames as `sweep\satellite\plaza-azNNN.png` with **no `artifacts/visual/` prefix**, so a basename lookup collides with the cartographic set and returns "0 of 6" — a lookup bug that reads exactly like a digest failure; match on the certificate's own `file` field. Second, a column-anomaly detector with no knowledge of the UI overlay will flag the style panel's own vertical edges at x = 1044 and x = 1254–1268 in all six frames as a render defect; those are the only vertical seams present in the plaza set. A strict speckle test over the same six frames found **zero** isolated-pixel speckle.

**Three more corrections from the same re-inspection, carried here because the sentences they correct live in artifacts and in `plan.md`.** (1) **"Nothing below luminance 8 in any frame" is a rounding artefact, not a fact.** 0.000–0.022% of each frame *is* below 8 — 0–31 small components — and 97–100% of that was below 8 pre-batch too, so a one-decimal summary of a small population was read as a categorical. The shape of the error is worth more than the number: a rounded share of a small population must never be quoted as a categorical floor (`artifacts/inspection-final2/REPORT-satellite-block.md:158,173`). (2) **The az060 shadow-side plane is still textureless, and "matte" understates it.** It reproduces exactly — mean 28.3, spread 3.2 against that frame's own sky control of 2.1 — and its 5×5 local-sd median/p90 is **0.00 / 0.34, identical to the same frame's clear sky** (0.00 / 0.34), with a cross-preset fine-band correlation of **0.022 against the same sky patch's −0.025** where material surfaces on the same run read 0.70–0.98. So a large surface in the delivered frames is statistically indistinguishable from sky in its texture. It is unchanged by this batch, it is not blocking, and the plausible cause is ambient-only lighting on a shadow face (`REPORT-satellite-block.md:165`). (3) **"The road's change is not detectable at block distance" holds, and not for the reason previously implied.** The lane measured the marking-free flattest 48×48 road interiors at that framing: **0.18–1.73 lv of fine structure with neighbour steps of 0.02–0.49 lv**, and the plaza's cross-preset ratio (0.28–0.29) does **not** transfer — block gives 0.30–1.31, median about 1.0 — because a 48 px window there is 6–12 m and already mip-averaged and the cartographic cells sit at 75–209 lv where one level is 0.5–1.3% of the mean. So **neither preset clears one level in its cleanest interior at that framing**: the reason is a bound on the framing and not a property of the material, which the structure's own cross-preset correlation confirms (r = 0.979 at az120 `left-ground` asphalt, 0.834 az180 street, 0.699 az000 junction; `REPORT-satellite-block.md:147-149`).
