## Findings

1. **P1 — Verify the original road point and bound every stitch.** `tools/data/support_source3.py:567–573`, `validate_local_geometry`, compares `replacement_native_cm` to the output and merely requires finite barycentrics summing to one. It never evaluates the barycentrics against the named original road triangle, requires their membership in the source face, compares an `original_native_cm`, or bounds displacement. The actual frozen validator accepted weights `[-100,101,0]` with a replacement translated 10 metres, returning `changed_face_coverage: true` in a tiny isolated fixture. Root confirmed the approved change is an explicit stitch of at most 1 mm. Evaluate in-range source barycentrics on exact original native coordinates; independently bind the original and replacement positions and enforce the displacement limit. Clarify the ledger kind so a moved replacement does not claim to be pure original-face interpolation. Keep inferred bank-anchored faces separate. Add actual-validator refusals for fabricated original coordinates, out-of-range weights, mismatched replacement coordinates and an over-limit displacement, with a genuine permitted stitch positive.

2. **P1 — Give road witnesses one consumed-GLB face identity.** `tools/data/support_source3.py:443–463` copies a road hit's aggregate index and rewrites `triangle` only for inferred/changed-road kinds. An unchanged road profile therefore keeps its index across all 25 roads rather than its consumed road GLB. A direct call of the actual `witness` retained aggregate `triangle: 100` while the selected road's `layer_ref.triangle` was 2. Changed-road witness/ray paths at `:637–647` instead copy primitive-local `triangle`; `surface_network.py:491–492` does the same for ordinary road rays. Resolve the flattened face ordinal within the selected consumed GLB deterministically from mesh/primitive/face ordering, while retaining primitive-local indices in `layer_ref`/`face_map`. Do not assume equality for multiple primitives. Cover an unchanged hit in a later road source, a changed hit and a road with multiple primitives through the actual profile and ray paths.

3. **P1 — Admit source lineage at the actual reader/reprofile boundary.** `tools/data/support_source3.py:386–439`, called by `Surface` and `--reprofile-frozen`, reconstructs output attributes/indices but never invokes `validate_local_geometry`. Only producer `prepare` calls that admission at `:680`. Thus a reader can emit the new inferred provider label without checking its actual raw bank anchors, declared-region confinement, deletion coverage and changed-road source anchors. Exact output readback proves correspondence to the ledger, not truth of those ledger claims. Reuse the parsed parent/current arrays to run the deterministic source/geometry admission before returning a Source3 `Surface` or publishing profiles. Avoid recursive Source3 initialization or reconstructing the Source2 terrain. Add a valid reader-path positive and refusals for mismatched inferred anchors, false road lineage and geometry outside its declared region.
