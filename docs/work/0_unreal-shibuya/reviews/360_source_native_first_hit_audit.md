## Finding

The source admission instrument does not model the actual native first-hit surface at the precision that matters here. Its current rejection is a reason to stop publication, but it is not sufficient evidence that the frozen route encounters a real stacked bridge or an unavoidable grade defect. A blanket road preference and a blanket highest-ground replacement are both unjustified by these observations.

Surface.check (surface_network.py:1080-1115) chooses the DEM-nearest PLATEAU road whenever its absolute DEM residual is <=1.5m, ahead of every rendered terrain face. It checks normal, then rejects any other road/terrain face >.2m and <=.8m above the chosen face. Native PhysicsSnapshot (AgentGeometry.cpp:214) traces the actual posed body/shoe/wheel point from +80cm to -100cm and takes LineTraceSingle's first blocking hit. It requires absolute gap<=20cm and normal.Z>=.7. These are different selection operations. The frozen XY path is legal OSM ground authority; road-face provenance does not independently establish that a road triangle is that way's physical layer.

The current Source2 policy explicitly removes terrain above road interiors and restores original terrain within250 micrometres of the road union exterior. Terrain below elevated decks remains. Therefore a narrow restored upper face can be a real rendered lip, rather than evidence that all upper terrain should replace a lower road. Ground OSM tags do not make the lip disappear, and DEM proximity alone does not prove native collision containment.

## Minimal defensible class repair and controlled evidence

First correct the source/native selection contract without changing scene geometry: exact actual native-cm containment and hit normals; select the first blocking hit in the same declared native vertical interval at every query. Separately require that selected hit to be an eligible ground support for the legal source path and correct mode. Ineligible first hits must refuse, never be ignored in favour of an eligible lower face. Preserve building-interior, bridge/tunnel/unknown-layer and directed vehicle interval restrictions; do not treat every nearest-DEM road or highest geometric hit as eligible ground. Preserve all470 IDs/order/legal XY and full populations, grade .2/.6, full-body dimensions, .2m gap, .7 normal and current frame/datum. Keep native posed validation independent.

This recommendation changes an instrument contract and needs design review before implementation. It is not a Source PASS. The existing Source2 restoration lip may still be a genuine discontinuity once exact containment is used; an exact first-hit selector must retain that failure if the lip is real.
