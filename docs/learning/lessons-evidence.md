## Bone corrections must match the delivered reference pose

**Anchor:** [Review 6](../work/0_shibuya-1km/reviews/6_implementation.md), accepted F6/F7, 2026-09-12. The reviewed controller is SHA-256 `368d7d6ce7cb913687c43ab3932e6dbafd6b0ce8d4a9407cf82fdde51410503c`; its rendered manifest is `182a0b635a91e9b991b4891fd953b99aa88855937e05c5ac8e48ed3521ecd29b`.

**Gate:** compare all drawn vertices with independently evaluated source geometry under an explicit quantization bound, then reconstruct the matching reference palette and test both zero correction and a controlled single-joint perturbation. Reintroducing the bind/idle reference mismatch must fail. This gate is not complete; the current probe is one near-LOD variant and no production repair is accepted.

The contact planner added corrected-bind skin minus bind skin to VAT that already contained an authored idle pose. Near-LOD head vertices carried about 35 mm of idle root drop, while dominant thigh/calf differences reached 109–136 mm and larger body residuals reached 431–576 mm. Soles still met the recorded flat-contact bounds across 378 cases: maximum planted drift 3.583476 mm and stance height 9.132385 mm. Those numbers remain valid within that instrument's scope; they did not validate the reference frame or full-body posture. Native full-mesh review exposed crouch and idle arms.

Zero correction alone can subtract the same wrong palette from itself and pass. Matching the evaluated Blender source, exact joint order, weights, mesh bind transforms, coordinate conversion and modifiers must precede that identity check. The 65 mm extra pelvis floor and fixed swing timing are separate F7 findings; removing the floor was not demonstrated as a complete repair and cannot substitute for the reference check.

## Process ancestry needs live identity

**Anchor:** Candidate 7b process-owner/process-cleanup evidence, 2026-09-12. Capture started 16:22:18 local. Windows process rows 8812/csrss and 4624/winlogon carried ParentProcessId 20264; 26020/fontdrvhost and 11712/dwm were children of 4624. All were created at 08:13:28 local. PID 20264 was later reused by a capture child. A PID-only transitive closure treated the old system rows as new descendants, and Stop-Process attempts were mislabeled as successful cleanup. Fresh inventory showed the four foreign processes still alive; they were preserved. No actual browser/server remained.

**Gate:** `test/process-ownership.test.ts` uses fake CIM rows only. The old closure explicitly admits all four wrong rows; the current classifier rejects older children, younger children of exited/reused parents and changed executable identities, while retaining positively known live orphans. Six tests pass on Windows, and the whole group is visibly skipped elsewhere. A missing helper initially looked like an empty result because PowerShell continued after an execution-policy error; the test now uses a process-local script policy and stops on loading errors, without changing machine policy. The reviewed wrapper must pin each live parent and actual child process handle, revalidate exact creation/name/path identity, and terminate through those held objects, never by resolving the PID again. Native capture with confirmed cleanup remains the additional live bound before this lesson can retire.

## A diagnostic must re-derive the tick's own arithmetic

**Anchor:** `artifacts/signature-routing/STALL.md` §5.3, 2026-09-18, from the walking-admission stall investigation. `stall-probe.ts`'s gate scan took the first plan gate ahead of the body's arc distance and ignored the held lease, so it reported slots 14 and 19's next gate as an occurrence of the same compound 40.6 m ahead and made same-compound re-entry an open hypothesis. The tick does not do that: a committed body is capped through `gateBeyond` (`src/agents/population/tick.ts:941-952`), which skips every gate of the compound the body is already inside. The instrument therefore named a gate the simulation never consults; the sentence is withdrawn in `BRIEF.md` and the hypothesis is falsified in its gate form (`STALL.md` §1), with the real cap a different authority 59.9–462.6 m ahead.

**Gate:** **owed, not built.** A check that any instrument re-deriving gate or planner arithmetic calls the same helpers the tick calls — or, failing that, an assertion that the instrument's derived gate matches the one the tick acted on. The mutation it must catch is preserved as `stall-probe.ts`'s own `nextGate` helper: re-derive the next gate ahead from the plan alone, without the lease, and the diagnostic must go red on a committed body inside a multi-occurrence compound. The wrong answer was plausible — 40.6 m ahead, in the compound the body was standing in, is what the frozen geometry looks like from outside — so nothing in the numbers contradicted it; only reading `gateBeyond` did.

## A lane probe calls the repo's pixel helper rather than re-deriving the stride

**Anchor:** `artifacts/inspection-final2/REPORT-satellite-overhead.md:31,141`, 2026-09-18, the satellite-overhead re-inspection. That lane's first `diff` blob matcher indexed a per-pixel mask with a **byte** offset, so it sampled every fourth pixel and reported **no difference at any of the fourteen terrain rims** — a plausible negative result that would have been written as "the cap changed nothing". A `pixel` probe disagreed with it at a named coordinate (a pixel at (1025,650) went `rgb(22,24,33)` to `rgb(42,41,42)`, delta 20); the lane fixed the index to `mask[offset / 4]` and re-ran, and every number it published is from the fixed run, with the fault disclosed. This is the **second** instance of the byte/pixel-stride class in this repository after `tools/flicker/judge.ts`'s `channelDelta` (see the defect register's flicker entry), and it arrived in a different author's instrument *after* the original was found and fixed. The failure mode is specific and worth naming: the wrong instrument reports a **negative** — absence of change — which reads as evidence of absence rather than as a broken ruler.

**Gate:** **owed, not built.** The mutation it must catch is preserved by the class itself: index a per-pixel mask with a byte offset and the probe must go red on a known change. The rule that would have prevented it needs no new arithmetic — the repository already has five adjacent pixel-difference helpers that multiply by four, verified by round 35 at `tools/post-chain/motion.spec.ts:94`, `tools/style-ui/support.ts:371`, `tools/flythrough/flythrough.spec.ts:240`, `tools/populated/diff.ts:49` and `tools/render-defects/frame-regions.ts:119` — so a lane probe should **call one of them rather than re-derive the stride**, the same shape as the rule above. The counter-example is in the same repository: `tools/inspect/crops.ts:668-677` refuses by name to write crops into a directory that already holds a different frame rather than guessing at an ambiguous input. The rule is carried in `docs/policies/local-rules.md`.

## A probe that agrees with itself is not evidence

**Anchor:** `artifacts/inspection-final2/REPORT-satellite-plaza.md:153-155,181`, 2026-09-18. The satellite-plaza lane built a rim-projection instrument whose internal checks all passed — the camera target landed at exactly (640, 360), a point 10 m above the target landed higher, all 134 scene bounding boxes parsed as axis-aligned — and which nonetheless **disagreed with the frames**: under that pose the horizon sits within ~50 px of frame centre and Shibuya Scramble Square's roof projects to screen y ≈ −384, far above the frame, while the frame plainly has a tall building rising to the top edge on the left. The lane discarded the instrument, recorded that its output is not evidence, and rested the rims answer on the pixels alone. What settled the projection basis elsewhere was **agreement with recorded pixels and between independent instruments**: a sibling lane's own forward projector put H1 at (782.4, 318.4) at depth 609.1 m against this lane's (782, 318) at 609 m, all fourteen apexes agreeing to a pixel, and two lanes independently reproduced the pre-cap hole positions to about 1 px. The same lane's published-markings control is what chose the azimuth convention at all (four candidates tested, only one landed the known-visible zebra on bright pixels).

**Gate:** **owed, not built.** A projection or re-derivation instrument has to be validated against a recorded outcome, not against its own internal invariants: the mutation to catch is a self-consistent basis that disagrees with the pixels, and the check is that the instrument reproduces a known pixel or a second independent instrument's answer on the same input. Read together with "A diagnostic must re-derive the tick's own arithmetic" above: both are a probe that agrees with itself and is wrong.
