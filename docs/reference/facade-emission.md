Runtime assigns eligibility from the verified tile plus the batch table's GML identifier, never from a numerical batch index. Unbound LODs are explicitly unlit. A changed selected tile fails with source re-audit guidance. The small eligibility vertex attribute is included in normal geometry memory accounting; no new texture is allocated.

The shader then requires the UV polygon, outward-normal dot at least 0.995 and distance at most 0.03 m from the observed source plane. Source reconstruction reproduced 86 runtime ray positions within 0.012723 m and UVs within 0.000021882; this is a bounded source/runtime reconstruction discrepancy, not an exact-coordinate claim. The three selected corner sets depart from their live planes by at most 0.004096 m. The 30 mm slab admits that measured uncertainty without admitting adjacent roof or facade planes. It is an authored eligibility tolerance and does not change world geometry or support.
