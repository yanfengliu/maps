# Bounded pavement presentation recipe

`npm run data:pavements` and the pavement stage of `npm run data:scene` use `tools/scene/pavement-recipe.ts`. The recipe reads raw PLATEAU road and bridge CityGML plus the built terrain and road meshes. It does not read recovery artifacts. It writes `data/scene/pavements.mesh`, `pavement-source.json` and `pavement-recipe.json`, without deleting sibling scene outputs.

The extracted producer reproduces the reviewed Candidate 5 presentation exactly: SHA-256 `3b24c5ef71f864fc6a90c768d8850b2f72ba1eb41255cf40dc37fd940115fb62`, 13,068,136 bytes and 464,525 triangles. It moved from `5d2a915047b0e8719e624f14f600c81c0a6c6342c4e8ce5b383bdfa987bcbc24` (12,974,860 bytes, 461,912 triangles) when the terrain cap re-bound its two mesh inputs (`terrain.mesh` `fef57d09…` to `c39d49ed…`, `roads.mesh` `3fe126cd…` to `0d429c31…`): the ground the source paths are measured against is now closed, so 2,613 triangles of pavement that had no support before are built. `pavement-source.json` did not move. That re-binding is review 34's item 1. The previous adaptive base also reproduces exactly, SHA-256 `bf44e124e2ba5b274f9e6babad60210bb6fa88f6e375477ab77b4aeb548acf49`. Full-file equality and separate position, normal and index buffer checks are recorded in `artifacts/recovery-realism-20260908/pavement-recipe-replay/replay.json`. This is reproduction of the bounded native-reviewed presentation, not a new whole-city support claim.

## Source and layer scope

Source selection retains all LOD3 polygons, records exact full-XYZ same-level aliases, and subtracts only same-parent LOD3 coverage from LOD2. The publication keeps 66,538 pieces, including 1,599.561189074 m² of mixed-parent LOD2 fallback. Raw source elevations remain in that publication. The four road CityGML files, the one bridge file, terrain and road mesh digests are bound in the recipe. Changed bytes fail with a source-review instruction before publication.

The two reviewed ground-level parents are `tran_f2a5948b-23c4-4932-83a2-a5c3591bf06c` and `tran_4b2989fd-ca82-459e-b88a-b1edc013862e`. Their 751 selected pieces are partitioned against actual terrain and road triangles. The existing ground support rule is retained: source and terrain plus 0.235 m, and an eligible highest road plus 0.08 m when it lies less than 0.75 m above terrain. The producer checks each source triangle's area residual against `max(1e-6 m², area × 1e-10)`. Exposed closure edges are noded only within their assigned layer, not globally by X/Z. The mesh pool retains full XYZ and normal identity.

The lower passage is only source polygon `poly_b6334821-d28d-446a-b791-33ee343ef14a`, clipped against polygons `poly-3b73ad3b-ec53-4a60-85a1-4ba8415613c4` and `poly-6b079e30-bf3e-4752-8263-e4c52e306bad` belonging to bridge `brid_cb8a47ec-def6-47a9-9366-b294a047ee63`. Its original source plane is retained where those bridge planes prove at least 0.5 m of vertical separation. The remaining source fragment remains explicitly unclassified. [Review 4](../work/0_shibuya-1km/reviews/4_design.md) records the independent raw-source check and the limited applicability of the upper TIN/GSI oracle there. The producer does not lower terrain or lift this passage to hide it.

Only these footprints replace parts of the prior adaptive presentation. Other rendered regions retain their prior geometry; their physical-layer interpretation is not accepted by this recipe. The source publication, base off-terrain count, exact replacement masks, per-triangle removed/retained area, source aliases, lower remainder and Float32 encoding ledger are kept in the output reports. This does not admit network walking heights or classify every low source surface. Geometry remains bounded to 16 MB and 500,000 triangles; exceeding either requires investigation.

## Separate physical paint overlay

The runtime paint renderer samples the unchanged road/pavement meshes near each source path elevation. Selection is limited to 0.5 m; a nearby 0.10 m top presentation allowance prevents paint burial in overlapping surfaces. Subdivision at most 0.25 m checks actual triangle edge slopes (at most 0.5 rise/run) and midpoint/centroid support residuals (at most 0.04 m) under a 0.06 m paint lift. It omits complete unsupported parts with explicit source IDs/reasons. These are finite checks, not proof of entire footprint coverage.

Only strict misses between opposed, bracketing and nearly parallel actual triangle edges may use the measured paint-only seam allowance: width at most 0.010 m, edge-height agreement at most 0.001 m, both heights within the source-elevation bound. Uses retain actual source edge IDs. The source meshes, graph and all pedestrian/vehicle support queries are unchanged. `test/paint-support.test.ts` covers stacked levels, wrong direction, one-sided boundaries, larger gaps, interior holes/ridges, the real former 7.545902 m paint sheet and all source-painted features. The accepted eight native hero views cover the central diagonal and three omitted endpoints only at crossing/approach framings; wider visual coverage remains pending.
