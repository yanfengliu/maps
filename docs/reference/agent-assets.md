## Runtime contract

`src/world/agent-assets.ts` defines the versioned manifest. Each generated human has separate near, medium and far geometry, a GLB with `_VAT_ID` on every exported vertex, and position/normal vertex-animation textures. That attribute preserves the source lookup when glTF splits a vertex at a UV seam. All levels use the same clip layout and stay animated.

Positions are metres, +Y is up, the origin is at the feet, and forward is +Z. Rotation is yaw about +Y. Textures are raw little-endian RGBA16F. Each animation frame occupies `rowsPerFrame` whole texture rows; `textureWidth` and `textureHeight` describe the padding. Runtime phase is normalized to [0, 1). Walk phase advances by distance travelled divided by the manifest's full two-step stride, including any per-agent scale. Idle advances by elapsed time divided by its duration. A transition between them must preserve stance rather than resetting the actor's distance.

The walk has 32 sampled frames, nominal duration 1 second and stride 1.1 metres. Idle has 16 sampled breathing frames over 2.5 seconds. The animation values describe the local mesh; the simulation supplies position, heading and travelled distance. Style changes alter appearance without changing these values.

Generated sources, GLBs, manifests and textures live under ignored `data/scene/agents/`. Native review frames and their digests live under ignored `artifacts/agents/`. `node tools/agents/build.ts` builds the pipeline. `node tools/agents/verify.ts` validates the generated contract. The latter names its bound: it does not establish natural motion, good likeness, crowd behaviour or frame rate.

## What the prototype exposed

The first clothed human exported correctly and still looked wrong. A 60-degree extra upper-arm rotation pushed arms inside the torso, and the source bind pose's forward-bent forearms made the hands hang in front of the body. Native front, profile and rear renders exposed both. The pose now aligns the forearms from their actual upper-arm direction and uses a smaller shoulder correction. Root independently inspected the corrected 720×960 frames and accepted the source and export path as viable, while leaving final motion and crowd acceptance open.

The first numerical gait check failed: at an endpoint, the requested ankle was 0.030594 metres beyond the reachable 0.753371-metre leg chain. The gate requires less than 0.015 metres of target error. Keeping the pelvis low enough through that endpoint reduced the dense 32-pose diagnostic's worst error to 0.00004744 metres; the threshold was not changed. The full bake records ankle target error and stance-foot world drift after adding the actor's forward travel. Generated VAT, the complete walking sequence and the eventual runtime shader still need to agree on that measurement.

Known quality risks remain: the initial studio lighting washed out facial detail; open hands and stiff arms need motion review; and three still poses do not prove planted feet. Full crowd acceptance also needs outfit/body diversity, visual checks of all geometry levels at their intended distances, and measured performance at 3,000 pedestrians alongside 200 vehicles.

The first delivered-position check caught a defect the rig check missed: actual exported `shoes01` vertex 17864 moved 0.052393 metres during stance while the ankle stayed planted. The verifier now follows ground-level vertices from the half-float VAT bytes after adding actor travel, with the same 0.005-metre drift limit for every variant and LOD. Imported calf weights bent the soles. Rigid foot weights fix the soles, but applying them to the whole shoe asset made its included tall sock cuffs separate from the legs. Native frames caught that second defect. The recipe keeps the visible soles rigid and removes the hidden tall cuffs under the current long trousers. These source corrections remain subject to the final native sequence and runtime review.

`src/world/agent-poses.ts` is the approved shared simulation/renderer interface. Core owns fixed stable slots and matching dense typed arrays for previous/current XYZ, yaw, travelled distance and spawn generation, plus active state, speed, scale and variant. The renderer only reads them. It interpolates heading by the shortest arc and takes current state directly when generation changes. LOD selection never changes the active population.

## Vehicle source investigation

The public grspro Kei Truck listing offers a 12.9k-triangle model under CC Attribution. Its page identifies it as a model authored from a photograph. Download access was not established in this environment, so it is not a pinned input. [Provider listing](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/kei-truck-13dc5e2aad2e4d00af57e8d99d7095e9).

The VRC-IW Toei Isuzu Erga bus listing is explicitly CC Attribution-NonCommercial, so it was not selected for this reusable pipeline. No free, directly retrievable JPN Taxi model with verified suitable licensing was found in this bounded search. The implemented route is original detailed Blender geometry for the required Japanese mix, with no downloaded brand textures. Vehicle acceptance remains open. [Bus provider listing](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/japanese-bus-toei-bus-tokyo-654cd8ac7cc84c2e9b1ceb14ca0e6dee).

The approved original recipe is `tools/agents/build-vehicles.py`: kei hatchback, tall taxi and city bus, in fixed class order 0/1/2. These are unbranded original vehicle types, not replicas or downloaded JPN Taxi assets. Their generated manifest distinguishes nominal body dimensions from measured full geometry bounds and collision envelopes, and supplies front/rear axle offsets and wheel radii. The simulation consumes that manifest rather than restating the dimensions.

Root rejected the first vehicle candidate after native front/profile/rear inspection: front and rear glass were occluded by cabin metal, smooth normals made flat body panels look lumpy, and lamps protruded as white slabs. The replacement follows the cabin slope for glazing, weights panel normals, adds wheel-well liners and fits lamp assemblies into the nose. The rejected first candidate remains at ignored `artifacts/agents/vehicles-rejected-v1`, with manifest SHA-256 `45288f25268db868f2de3285fdc359a4b8f1e04bbbe3b34cb6e6bce3de348287` and recipe `bc0dc6e98b36eb5378ec082ae92fcebd2520a9987fa6c36e33098e7247f9706f`. Root's old taxi/bus six-frame digest check matched; kei was regenerated during inspection, so its original three bytes were recovered and verified in this preserved candidate. New review handoffs use a frozen candidate directory and manifest, never a currently regenerating output path.

## Shipping renderer and remaining integration evidence

`src/agents/render/agents.ts` consumes the approved dense pose buffers. It instances actual generated geometry by variant, material and distance LOD, without reducing the active population. Source geometry after glTF seam expansion has 18,970–22,322 near vertices, 2,459–2,888 medium vertices and 500–523 far vertices per variant. The distance thresholds are 18 and 60 metres. These choices need populated performance measurement; 3,000 detailed near meshes would exceed the intended budget.

The first actual shipping-shader preview exposed a defect absent from Blender stills: MPFB exported every layer as alpha blending, so instanced hair and garments sorted incorrectly and developed large holes. The renderer now uses depth-writing alpha cutouts at 0.45. Cartographic changes RGB while preserving the same alpha mask. The rejected preview remains at `artifacts/agents/candidates/shader-1788891635479`. Its replacement, `shader-1788892188751`, captured 24 native 1280×720 frames with no browser errors; manifest SHA-256 is `b219a49e73bdd24bc5d73ee1af2aafb45d82f1f515cacfe2941203c2909501b6`. The close views inspected by the asset worker show intact hair and clothing in both styles. This six-actor fixture is not a simulation, full-population, shadow, AO or frame-rate check.

Production GTAO uses a normal-material override, which originally bypassed the human deformation. `src/agents/render/vat.ts` now supplies one deformation to main color, directional/spot shadow depth, point-light distance and registered AO normal materials. The normal material also keeps UV0 texture-alpha and alpha-map-green cutouts, source side and depth state. `src/agents/render/pass-materials.ts` registers only actual VAT meshes and removes registrations on disposal. The rendering worker owns the GTAO adapter and its exception-safe restoration. `test/agent-render-passes.test.ts` checks actual HumanRenderer wiring over all nine variant/LOD groups against upstream shader inputs. It is CPU wiring coverage; integrated moving shadow and AO pixels still need inspection.

### A0 fixed reproduction and disqualifiers

The original delivered-data reproduction is preserved in `artifacts/agents/review2/probe-result.json`: the speed-weighted walk/idle mix moves a stopped near-LOD sole 0.275146 m and produces 0.257874–0.257904 m of drift at steady 0.1 m/s. `artifacts/agents/repair-review2-baseline/` records a fresh reproduction before the repair. A new phase-corresponding settled-strip proposal was tested before baking with `node artifacts/agents/settle-design/probe.mjs`. Its immutable source inputs are the original-asset snapshot above. The probe gives the settled strip ideal zero-height soles and reads actual walk VAT, selected-scene triangles and fractional texture samples.

The settled-strip probe's fixed matrix is three variants × three LODs × ten initial phases (`0`, `0.03125`, `0.125`, `0.249`, `0.49`, `0.5`, `0.53125`, `0.625`, `0.749`, `0.99`) × three restart speeds (`0.1`, `1.1`, `2` m/s), at 240 Hz with a 0.2 s blend. It measures both soles until their first millimetre of lift rather than choosing contact from the old walk phase. Worst near-ground horizontal movement is 29.616 mm before 0.931 mm of clearance, 12.5 ms after a phase-zero restart at 2 m/s. This is a counterexample to treating phase correspondence alone as planted-foot control. The positive clearance does not prove literal surface contact; it identifies a near-ground transition needing explicit contact/release semantics and visual judgment. The original 5 mm planted drift and 15 mm stance-height bounds are unchanged.

A candidate must be scored on the same authored body trajectories without changing them. It must cover arbitrary and fractional stop phases, restart during settling, repeated short pauses, steady slow motion and acceleration, at every variant and LOD. Required observations are actual drawn sole coordinates, world ground heights, contact/release events and continuous body poses. Both feet must settle to ground when stopped; a lifted moving foot must visibly clear the ground before substantial travel. A new contact classifier must be independently reviewed against the geometric observations before it can replace the instrument above. Final acceptance additionally requires the shipping main, depth, distance and AO passes over motion, natural street appearance, world support and measured full-population cost.

Disqualifiers are: changing body XYZ/travel or its clock to accommodate animation; relabeling a grounded foot as swinging solely because of an old phase; hiding feet or reducing active population; leaving a stopped foot raised indefinitely; an abrupt pose jump or near-ground drag disguised by a blend; weakening the established numeric bounds; measuring ideal bone targets instead of delivered drawable soles; omitting fractional phases, quick reversals, variants or LODs; and moving the unresolved foot-lock problem behind an unverified IK or animation-library call. A fixed 1.1 m distance-to-phase mapping is replaceable if evidence supports a deterministic, travel-driven alternative. No third blend variation or full bake is accepted without a distinct mechanism and an independently checked contact instrument.
